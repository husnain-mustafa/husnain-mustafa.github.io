# Design System: Husnain Mustafa Portfolio

## Reading
High-end personal portfolio for recruiters, engineering leaders, and enterprise clients evaluating expertise in Business Intelligence, Applied AI, and Cloud Data Architecture.

## Aesthetic Direction
* **Vibe**: Refined Minimalist Editorial + High-Tech Craft.
* **Palette**: 
  - Base: Warm Near-Black (`#09090b`, `zinc-950`)
  - Elevated Surfaces: `zinc-900/60` with clean 1px borders (`zinc-800/80`)
  - Accent: Saturated Emerald (`#10b981`) & Precision Slate Blue (`#38bdf8`) — strict lock across all sections.
  - Typography: Geist Sans & Geist Mono with strong weight contrast.
* **Layout Discipline**:
  - Restrained eyebrows (max 1 eyebrow per 3 sections).
  - Explicit mobile collapse for all multi-column sections.
  - Interactive proof points over static cards.
* **Dials**: `DESIGN_VARIANCE: 7` | `MOTION_INTENSITY: 5` | `VISUAL_DENSITY: 4`
